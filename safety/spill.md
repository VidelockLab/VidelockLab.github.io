# Spill

---

## First, which kind?

| Where | Do this first |
|---|---|
| **Inside a biosafety cabinet** | Leave the cabinet **running**. Do not switch it off. |
| **Outside a cabinet, in the lab** | Get everyone out, close the door, **wait 30 minutes** for aerosols to settle. |
| **Outside the lab** — corridor, lift, public area | **Do not clean it up.** Keep people away and call **(310) 825-9797**. |
| **Anything you are not equipped for** | Call **(310) 825-9797**. There is no penalty for asking. |

---

## Inside a biosafety cabinet

1. Keep the cabinet running.
2. Cover the spill with absorbent material.
3. Apply 10% bleach and leave the **full 10-minute contact time**.
4. Wipe up, then wipe with 70% ethanol to remove bleach residue.
5. Dispose of everything used as biohazardous solid waste.
6. Tell the PI and log it.

---

## Outside a biosafety cabinet

1. Tell everyone to leave. Close the door. Post a warning sign with your name and number.
2. **Wait 30 minutes.** Do not shortcut this — it is how aerosols settle.
3. Put on fresh PPE: gown, double gloves, eye protection.
4. Pick up broken glass and sharps with **tongs, forceps, or a brush and dustpan** — never hands — into a sharps container.
5. Cover the spill *and the whole splash zone* with absorbent.
6. Apply 10% bleach, full contact time.
7. Dispose of absorbent as biohazardous solid waste.
8. Disinfect all surfaces again.
9. Remove PPE, wash hands.
10. Tell the PI, report to **(310) 825-9797**, and log it.

## In a shared space

A spill in a shared fume hood or shared equipment room affects people who are not in this lab and who may not know what was spilled.

- Tell every person present, immediately
- Post a sign on the equipment saying what was spilled, when, and a contact number
- Do not release the space back to shared use until decontamination is complete and the sign comes down
- Tell the other regular users afterwards, even once it is cleaned up

---

## A container dropped in transit

**This is the one spill nobody cleans up themselves.**

1. Do not pick anything up with your hands.
2. Keep people away — but stay nearby yourself. You are the only person who knows what was in it.
3. Call **(310) 825-9797**. After hours call **9-1-1** — UCPD holds the 24/7 on-call numbers for EH&S staff.
4. Tell responders exactly what the container held.

---

## Disinfectant

**10% household bleach**, made fresh, **10-minute contact time**.

70% ethanol is *not* a permitted disinfectant for human blood or other potentially infectious material in California. It is used only to remove bleach residue after the contact time has elapsed.
