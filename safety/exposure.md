# Exposure

Splash, needlestick, cut, or anything on skin or in eyes.

---

## Do this now

**1. Flush. Fifteen minutes. Start before you do anything else.**

- Skin, cut, or puncture — soap and water, 15 minutes
- Eye or mucous membrane — eyewash, 15 minutes, continuously

Someone else makes the calls while you are at the eyewash. Do not stop flushing to find a phone.

**2. Tell everyone in the room**, so nobody else walks into it.

**3. Remove contaminated PPE**, turning exposed surfaces inward, into a leak-proof bag.

**4. Go for medical care.** Take the Post-Exposure Plan for whatever you were working with.

| | |
|---|---|
| Mon–Fri 7 a.m.–4 p.m. | Occupational Health, CHS 17-240 — **(310) 825-6771** |
| Nights, weekends, holidays | Ronald Reagan Emergency Room, 757 Westwood Plaza — **(310) 825-2111** |

Say it is an occupational laboratory exposure and needs immediate attention.

**5. Tell the PI.**

**6. Report to the EH&S Biosafety Hotline within 8 hours** — **(310) 825-9797**, option 3. Leave a voicemail if nobody answers.

**7. Log it** in the lab's Incident and Near Miss Log.

---

## Time limits that actually bite

| | |
|---|---|
| **Lentiviral vector** | Post-exposure prophylaxis must start **within 2 hours**. Take the lentivirus Post-Exposure Plan with you. |
| Bloodborne pathogen exposure | Most effective within hours. Go immediately. |
| Reporting to EH&S | 8 hours |
| Workers' Compensation paperwork | 24 hours |

**If you do not know which agent was involved, treat it as the most hazardous one it could have been, and go now.**

---

## Tell the clinician

- What you were working with, and what else was on the sharp — a needle used in an animal, or in a culture containing another agent, may carry the risk that matters most
- Any medication you take or condition you have that affects treatment
- That the UCLA Institutional Biosafety Committee considers treatment for recombinant viral vector exposure emergent

---

## Serious injury or fire

**Call 9-1-1 first.** Give first aid. Warn responders if there is a risk of exposure to infectious agents so they can protect themselves. Then report to EH&S at (310) 825-9797.
