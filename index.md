The Videlock Lab studies intestinal pathogenic mechanisms in Parkinson's
disease — working toward gut biomarkers for early diagnosis, and gut-directed
therapies that could slow progression during the premotor phase.

Principal investigator: **Elizabeth (Beth) Videlock, MD, PhD**, Health
Sciences Assistant Clinical Professor of Medicine. The lab is housed within
the UCLA Center for Inflammatory Bowel Diseases, in the MacDonald Medical
Research Laboratory, Room 1526.
